# Security

Please report a security problem privately: open this repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
Don't open a public issue or discussion for it.

Say what you found, the version (the tag, or `EXPORTED_FROM` in a checkout), the steps that show it, and what it lets
someone do. You'll get an answer there, and a fix ships in a new release.

Thimbleby runs on your own computer and listens only on that computer. Reports about the app reaching beyond that, about
anything it sends without your approval, about another person's data showing, or about the add-on catalog's signature are
the most welcome.
