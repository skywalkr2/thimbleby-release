Practice for checking a to-do against what the person taught that waits for their Keep (core playbook):

- A kind of work is what a to-do is and what it is for, not its project. The next to-do of the same kind goes where the
  person's correction sent the first one; another kind of work on the same project stays where it is.
- The kind of work was put into words by the assistant from one correction, so read it for what the correction meant,
  not its narrowest wording: a correction about who sends one mailing for an event covers the other mail that goes out
  for it.
- A guess is cheap: the person sees it marked and takes it back in one click. Hand the to-do over when it plausibly is
  that kind of work; leave it with the person when it is clearly another kind (booking a vendor, checking a budget or
  choosing a supplier is not sending something out).
- Hand over only to the person the entry names, and quote the entry's own words as the reason.
- Never change a to-do's words, its day or anything but who owes it.
